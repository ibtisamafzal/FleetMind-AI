# Driver Safety and Operations Policy (Pakistan Fleet Guidelines)

## Section 1: Speed and Driving Behavior (NH&MP Regulations)
- Commercial speed limit: Urban city limits maximum 50 km/h; National Highways (N-5, N-55) maximum 80 km/h; Motorways (M-2, M-3, M-5, M-9) maximum 90 km/h for heavy commercial trucks and 100 km/h for light commercial vans, compliant with National Highway & Motorway Police (NH&MP) directives.
- Any sustained speed exceeding 100 km/h on commercial freights triggers an automated high-risk incident alert.
- Drivers with an average speed above 70 km/h in dense urban delivery hubs (Karachi, Lahore, Rawalpindi) must complete route coaching.

## Section 2: Rest Breaks and Hours of Service
- Maximum continuous driving time without a rest break is 4.5 hours on national motorway transits.
- Mandatory rest interval: 45 minutes pause at designated NH&MP Motorway Service Areas (e.g. Sukheki, Bhera, Sial).
- Maximum daily driving limit: 9 hours per 24-hour cycle to prevent driver fatigue.

## Section 3: Driver Performance Rating & Remediation
- Rating metric range: 1.00 to 5.00 stars based on on-time delivery rate, zero accident record, and telematics smoothness.
- Drivers with a delay rate greater than 18% over their last 50 trips are scheduled for routing optimization retraining.
