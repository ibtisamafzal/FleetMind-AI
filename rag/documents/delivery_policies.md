# Delivery and Dispatch Policies (Pakistan Operations)

## Section 1: Dispatch Allocation Guidelines
- Deliveries are categorized as: Standard Courier, Priority Express, or Heavy Cargo Freight (CPEC / National Corridors).
- Vehicles flagged with "HIGH" risk (Risk Score >= 70) must NOT be assigned to high-value priority routes or long-haul transits exceeding 100 km (e.g. M-2 Lahore-Islamabad or N-5 Karachi-Lahore).
- Severe weather in Pakistan: Winter dense smog/fog on Punjab Motorways (M-2, M-3, M-4) requires lead-time buffering of +45% or convoy dispatch. Extreme summer heat (>45°C in South Punjab & Sindh) requires daytime rest scheduling and night transit prioritization.

## Section 2: Delay Reporting and Mitigation
- Any transit with delay probability greater than 60% calculated by the ML delay predictor must be reviewed by the dispatch dispatcher before departure from Karachi Port, Lahore Sundar Hub, or Islamabad I-9.
- Recommended mitigations include:
  1. Splitting multi-stop manifests (> 4 stops in dense urban centers like Lahore, Karachi, Rawalpindi).
  2. Rescheduling departure time to avoid peak urban congestion hours (08:00–10:00 and 17:00–19:30).
  3. Reassigning experienced drivers (experience > 5 years) to night transit corridors and motorway routes.
