# Fleet Vehicle Specifications & Technical Reference

## Unit Spotlight: Volvo FL Electric (e.g. Unit VH-027, LEA-4823)
- **Type**: 16-tonne Box Truck, Zero Emissions Urban Distribution (Lahore Division).
- **Powertrain**: Dual electric motors, 200 kW continuous / 330 kW peak power.
- **Battery Capacity**: 265 kWh (4 x 66 kWh lithium-nickel-manganese-cobalt packs).
- **Nominal Range**: Up to 300 km on full charge under standard urban test cycle.
- **Service Cycle**: Every 20,000 km or 6 months. Exceeding 15,000 km without inspection triggers severe thermal and mechanical risk alerts.
- **Common Wear Points**: Pneumatic brake callipers, regenerative inverter cooling pumps, tire sidewall strain under maximum payload.

## Unit Spotlight: Ford E-Transit (Cargo Van)
- **Type**: 3.5-tonne Electric Cargo Van.
- **Battery**: 68 kWh usable lithium-ion.
- **Range**: 240–260 km WLTP.
- **Service Cycle**: Every 25,000 km.

## Unit Spotlight: Mercedes-Benz Sprinter 314 CDI (Diesel)
- **Type**: 3.5-tonne High-Roof Van, 2.1L Turbocharged Diesel OM651.
- **Service Cycle**: Every 20,000 km. Mandatory AdBlue replenishment every 6,000 km.
