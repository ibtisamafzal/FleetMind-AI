# Fuel & Energy Efficiency Guidelines

## Section 1: Standard Efficiency Baselines
- **Electric Cargo Vans (e.g., Ford E-Transit, Renault Master E-Tech)**: Expected baseline is 16.0 to 18.5 km/kWh equivalent (~18 km/L energy equivalent).
- **Electric Box Trucks (e.g., Volvo FL Electric, DAF LF Electric)**: Expected baseline is 13.5 to 15.5 km/L energy equivalent.
- **Diesel Heavy Box Trucks (e.g., MAN TGM, Scania)**: Expected baseline is 8.0 to 9.2 km/L.
- **Diesel Cargo Vans (e.g., Mercedes Sprinter)**: Expected baseline is 10.5 to 12.0 km/L.

## Section 2: Identifying and Resolving Fuel Anomalies
- **Sudden Drop (> 15%)**: A sudden drop in fuel efficiency exceeding 15% from the 30-day baseline indicates:
  1. Under-inflated tires or misaligned wheels.
  2. Fuel injector or diesel particulate filter (DPF) clogging in diesel units.
  3. Cell imbalance or thermal degradation in battery packs.
  4. Aggressive driver behavior (excessive idling > 15 minutes, rapid acceleration, sustained speeds above 90 km/h).
- **Manager Investigation Protocol**:
  1. Review driver telematics for excessive speeding and idle time.
  2. Inspect tire pressures immediately.
  3. Run an on-board diagnostic (OBD-II / CAN bus) scan for hidden powertrain error codes.
