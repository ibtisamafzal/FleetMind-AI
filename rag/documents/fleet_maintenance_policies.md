# Fleet Maintenance Guidelines & Standard Operating Procedures

## Section 1: Preventative Maintenance Intervals
- **Inspection Frequency**: All fleet vehicles must undergo a routine safety and diagnostics inspection every 10,000 km or every 90 days, whichever occurs first.
- **Critical Service Overhaul Threshold**: If a commercial box truck or cargo van exceeds 15,000 km since its last recorded service, the vehicle must be automatically flagged as "HIGH RISK" for mechanical fatigue and overdue maintenance.
- **Brake System Standards**: Minimum allowable brake pad thickness is 4.0 mm for vans and 6.0 mm for heavy trucks. Immediate rotor resurfacing or replacement is mandatory if pad thickness drops below 3.0 mm.
- **Tire Pressure and Tread**: Minimum commercial tire tread depth is 3.5 mm. Tire pressures must be validated weekly against manufacturer cold specifications.

## Section 2: Electric Fleet Maintenance Standards
- **Battery Health**: High-voltage battery pack state-of-health (SoH) below 82% triggers an engineering diagnostic review.
- **Cooling Loops**: Thermal management coolant loops must be flushed annually. High battery pack temperatures under standard driving conditions require vehicle grounding.
- **Regenerative Braking Calibration**: Ensure brake pad wear sensors and regenerative braking software are calibrated during every 20,000 km service cycle.

## Section 3: High-Risk Vehicle Protocol
- Any vehicle with an operational risk score exceeding 70/100 must not be dispatched on long-haul transit routes (> 100 km).
- Route assignment priority: High-risk vehicles are strictly restricted to local depot transfers or designated for shop inspection within 48 hours.
